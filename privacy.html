Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · November 18, 2026

Your recordings

When you start a session and allow microphone access, your Apple Watch detects speech and saves audio clips. Your watch sends them to your paired iPhone through Apple’s device connection. WatchRecorder stores audio, text, edits, tags and markers in your library. Transcription, search and the default text tools run on your device. You can use them without a WatchRecorder account. WatchRecorder does not use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.

Optional permissions

With the wrist gesture enabled, WatchRecorder uses motion samples on your watch to mark moments during recording. On iPhone, Calendar access lets you find nearby events and save a chosen event’s title, dates and identifier with a recording. Reminders access lets you add selected tasks, source notes and any due dates to your reminders account. Calendar and Reminders use your system account’s sync settings. You can change permissions in your device settings.

External text AI

This option starts turned off. If you supply an HTTPS service, model and API key, you can review the destination and consent to sending text. When you request a summary or tasks, WatchRecorder sends machine transcript text and citation identifiers directly to your service. Questions send your question and relevant passages with their citation identifiers. The provider receives your API key for authentication and can associate requests with your account. Your audio is excluded. Check the provider’s own retention and privacy terms before enabling it.

Changing your AI choice

The key is held in this iPhone’s Keychain. Open Settings → External Text AI to turn off future requests or choose Delete API Key and Turn Off. To erase text previously sent to a provider, use that provider’s deletion process.

Model downloads and sharing

The default speech model comes with the app. Downloading a model or retrieving a replacement connects to the model host at hf-mirror.com and its download infrastructure. They receive the file request and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. When you export, you choose which content to share and its destination. A diagnostic report you choose to share includes recording identifiers and file hashes, status, versions, timing and storage figures. It excludes audio, titles, transcripts, AI content and API keys.

Retention and deletion

You can move iPhone recordings to Recently Deleted and restore them for 30 days. Expired items are cleared when the app starts or returns to the foreground. You can permanently delete them earlier in Settings → Library & Storage → Recently Deleted. Local deletion records help prevent a recording from being reimported. The watch may discard transferred audio after the phone acknowledges receipt. Manage any exported files, reminders, device backups and provider copies separately.

Privacy questions

Use the App Support link on the WatchRecorder App Store page to reach the developer. For a question about a recording, share the app version and the steps you took. Keep audio, transcript text and API keys private.
