WatchRecorder Support

Help for the Apple Watch recorder and its iPhone companion.

Start a recording

Open WatchRecorder on your paired Apple Watch, tap to record and allow microphone access. “Waiting for speech” means the session is listening for speech to save. Use the stop button when you finish.

Find your words

On your iPhone, open Recordings and tap a date to expand its recordings. For a keyword, tap Search, tap the outlined field and enter a word or phrase. Find words searches the current transcript, title, tags and marker notes. By meaning works with the machine transcript.

Check a missing recording or transcript

Bring the watch and its paired iPhone within range and open WatchRecorder on the phone. If a recording is absent from the default list, change With transcript to All. Check the entry’s transfer and transcription status. Settings → Transcription & Models shows the queue and the selected model. Keep the existing watch and phone copies while a transfer is pending.

Edit, restore or share

Open a recording and tap Edit to correct the transcript. Keep the changes with Save. Export lets you choose the material and transcript version before using the system share sheet. To restore a deleted recording, open Settings → Library & Storage → Recently Deleted and choose Restore within 30 days.

Change external AI settings

To use only the local text tools, open Settings → External Text AI and turn it off. Delete API Key and Turn Off also removes the credential stored on this iPhone. A transcription model download does not require that credential.

Report a problem

Email 948425230@qq.com. Include the WatchRecorder version from Settings → Help & Diagnostics, your iOS and watchOS versions, and the steps that show the issue. If needed, Generate and Share Diagnostic Report creates a report you can review before choosing a recipient. Keep audio files, transcripts and API keys private.
